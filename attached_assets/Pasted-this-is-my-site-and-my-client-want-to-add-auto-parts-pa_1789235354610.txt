#### Car Acquisition

On the customer's request, our staff can place bids and purchase cars from usual auctions as well as recycle car auctions.

#### Documentation

Once the car arrives in our yard, we upload 30-40 photos and engine working video to customers' personal page.

#### Dismantling

We start dismantling once all cars for the container are collected. Our staff dismantle 5-10 cars daily based on difficulty.

#### Shipping Timeline

Usually container waiting time in Japan after loading is 1 week. Transit time by ship depends on destination (Average 30-35 days).
